What is Animocha Studio?

Animocha Studio is a powerful 2D animation software that is designed for iPhone, iPhone Duo, & iPads.


Portable, powerful 2D animation software. Now with collaboration!

Animocha Studio's killer feature is the ability to collaborate with other animators or artists within the same project!


Unique project formats & extra brewed goodness

Animocha Studio can save your projects as ".mocha" files, perfect for directly sharing with trusted friends! You can also marvelously export your masterpieces as your standard mainstream formats (.mp4, png, apng, gif, etc.).


To be avaliable for iOS & iPadOS, although it's sweetest with iPad & iPhone Duo!

Animocha Studio will not be exclusive to iPads, and as many core features as possible will be accessible on standard iPhones no older than iPhone 14*. That being said, the best experience will be on iPads and iPhone Duo. But we can't stop you from finding out creative ways to get the most out of Animocha Studio on any supported iOS/iPadOS device!

*This is subject to change though.

!Current project status!

Barebones, original desktop proof of concept:

This is a GIF of a MVP proof of concept made in Godot Engine. Originally, Animocha Studio was going to be a desktop app, but it's been decided that having Animocha as a mobile yet just as powerful app with the added benefits of being exclusively designed for iOS/iPadOS devices would be worth it and much more convenient.


The final version of Animocha Studio is aimed to be developed for iOS/iPadOS devices. For more screenshots, visit the MVP folder in the GitHub repository.


As of now, Animocha Studio is still in active development, with currently only one developer working on it! There are no social media accounts or community spaces in regards to this project, and to be honest, there likely won't ever be. For now, stay tuned!
